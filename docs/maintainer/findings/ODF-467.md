## ODF-467 — CI repair delegation skips reading another worktree’s overlapping edits

**Meaning:** The ownership scan before a CI repair finds another active worktree with uncommitted edits to the failing spec, but does not read them, so the repair duplicates a concurrent fix.

**Follow-up:** Open, unqueued.

**Identity and limits:** Open Dough / DD-475 → ODF-467. Unseen concrete issue in CI-monitor ownership handling. One execution; the duplicate was dropped before publication on the developer’s decision. Maintained source assessed at `220026a4e1b8409ffa81f50cd8e3130945ff8472` (0.3.93).

active worktree with uncommitted edits to the failing spec. It called them
nearby edits without reading them and delegated the repair. That worktree
published the same fix about an hour later; the duplicate surfaced only at the
refactor pass and was dropped on the developer's decision.
