# Identical prospective reviewer brief

Review only ASSIGNED_PACKET, its frozen common evidence and candidate code via
`git show <candidate>:<path>`. Do not enumerate sibling packets, inspect the other
arm, assessor reference or earlier reports. Read-only; no edits or duplicate tests.
Use the supplied frozen proof standard and report human-needed decisions to the
steward; do not solicit duplicate human answers.

Budget: finish within eight minutes of dispatch, including tool/queue waits.
Identify specific responsibilities and observing assertions; a generic caveat is
insufficient. Return each proof gap or unsupported acceptance/exclusion, M with
an audited denominator only when supplied, G, reviewer U beyond H0, acceptance
judgment, evidence limits, inspected inputs/tool counts and unfinished work.
Use unknown for unavailable denominators, cost, proof or discovery attribution.

## Scoring definitions from the frozen plan (revision 2; not used by probes)

- Missed consumer M: An audited required purpose neither covered by inspected
  sufficient proof nor specifically flagged as unresolved when that arm finishes.
  Unsupported exclusion or false completion counts. A generic caveat does not
  identify a gap. Record discovery, traversal, proof-selection or acceptance stage.
- Proof gap caught G: A specific missing or inadequate observer/proof obligation
  surfaced before acceptance, including a consumer already named in the raw match
  list. Keep separate from novel discovery.
- Useful discovery U: A validated purpose that changes justified proof, correction
  or exclusion. Reviewer credit requires addition beyond H0, once per purpose with
  the concrete decision. Restating a supplied lead is confirmation, not discovery.
- Report M / audited purposes only with an audited denominator; otherwise unknown.
  A specifically flagged gap does not also become a missed purpose for that arm.
  Fixture rows, historic match counts and passed tests do not supply a denominator.
