## ODF-408 — Agent-operable observation is handed to the developer first

**Meaning:** A planned evaluation or desktop observation that needs only local, unpaid access the agent can use is handed to the developer before the agent attempts it, costing round trips; when the developer is away or the session is locked, the observation may not happen at all.

**Follow-up:** Open, unqueued; not resolved.

**Identity / assessment (2026-11-03):** Two projects, two executions (Open Dough 0.3.81, Clipboard 0.3.82). Related to ODF-324 (native checks keep asking after attribution), ODF-307 and ODF-244, without a shared cause. Where the developer's own judgment is the promise, that judgment stays with the developer; Clipboard adds the unchecked lock/presence state. Maintained guidance assessed at `325f9de8a1f635a44b0071cf0660a2593a21f6b9`.
