## ODF-409 — CI failure inventory omits cancelled jobs whose step failed

**Meaning:** `ci-github-diagnostic.mjs failure` excludes `cancelled` jobs, so a job cancelled at the suite deadline after its own test step failed yields `inventory: {complete: true, failedJobs: 0}` for a `CI_INCOMPLETE` run.

**Follow-up:** Open, unqueued; not resolved.

**Identity / assessment (2026-11-03):** Defect in the inventory delivered for ODF-380 by SEED-208 (first contained in v0.3.80); a new linked identity, not ODF-380's bounded-excerpt mechanism. The shard deadline breach under slow runner setup is Open Dough's local DD-279 facet. One execution on 0.3.83. Maintained guidance assessed at `325f9de8a1f635a44b0071cf0660a2593a21f6b9`.

### Retained source evidence — open-dough / DD-411

**Source:** [open-dough / DearDough.md](https://github.com/terryyin/open-dough/blob/325f9de8a1f635a44b0071cf0660a2593a21f6b9/DearDough.md).

**Source mapping:** open-dough / DD-411 → ODF-409.

`ci-github-diagnostic.mjs failure` filters jobs through `isFailedJob`
(`ci-failures.mjs:214`), which excludes `cancelled`. A job cancelled at the
workflow's suite deadline, after its own test step failed, therefore yields
`inventory: {complete: true, failedJobs: 0, gaps: []}` for a `CI_INCOMPLETE`
run. That reads as "nothing failed" while the run has no verdict.
