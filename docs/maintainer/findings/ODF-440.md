## ODF-440 — Coordinator resolution requires existing tests to pass unchanged when they encode the changed timing

**Meaning:** After an owner decision on a timing-changing slice, a coordinator instructs the implementer that existing consumer tests must pass unchanged, even though three of those tests also assert pre-refresh state that held only under the old timing, causing a second implementer stop.

**Follow-up:** Open, unqueued; not resolved.
