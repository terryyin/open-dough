## ODF-462 — A plan’s consumer inventory is narrower than the story’s governing rule

**Meaning:** A plan builds its slice consumer lists from one literal search instead of the story’s governing rule, so consumers outside that search surface only at acceptance or in implementation.

**Follow-up:** Open, unqueued.

**Identity and limits:** Open Dough / DD-468 → ODF-462. Relationship to ODF-074 (unverified premises) and ODF-150 (indirect consumers) is uncertain: the premise was inspected, but with a search narrower than the rule. Two executions. Maintained source assessed at `220026a4e1b8409ffa81f50cd8e3130945ff8472` (0.3.93).
