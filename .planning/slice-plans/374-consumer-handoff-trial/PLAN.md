## Frozen definitions, comparisons and decision

Purpose=responsibility+changed/preserved observable+observing assertion, deduplicated
across equivalent files/assertions. Keep evaluation obligations separate from the
consumer denominator; report each reference's scope and completeness limits.
M=audited required purpose neither sufficiently proved by inspected evidence nor
specifically flagged unresolved at arm finish; generic caveat/false completeness
fails. G=specific proof gap caught, including an already listed consumer. U=validated
new purpose beyond H0 changing justified proof/correction/exclusion, once per
purpose; supplied lead restatements are confirmations. L=valid A discovery absent
B disposition or inspected equivalent proof. Compare purpose sets, not counts alone.
Report M/positive audited denominator, initial genuine omissions for gap detection,
unknowns, stage/severity and actual decision. No population-rate/significance claim.

R=native complete review task elapsed. T_A=C+P_A+R_A, T_B=C+P_B_total+R_B; include timed
amendments in their actual owners. C measured once, credited to both; B's extra
prep is never free. Report human interruptions/minutes, tool/reread work, union
proof/test/CI/rework and maintenance costs separately when actually observed.
