# SEED-401 first-report pilot assessment

The pilot closed on 22 November 2026. **Effectiveness was inconclusive. Keep the
current first-report rule and close the pilot.** The sample had three ordinary
completions and zero live uses of the candidate wording. The comparison could
establish neither a reduction in combined writing and triage effort nor a
change in consequential misses. The evidence supports no adoption or revision
story.

| Project | Ordinary completions | Current wording | Candidate wording | Process review skipped |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Doughnut | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| Pygardon | 2 | 0 | 0 | 2 |

Doughnut’s eligible review recorded one first finding about testing for a
competing listener on a readiness port, [DD-517](https://github.com/nerds-odd-e/doughnut/blob/3b37cd28f0b1ad0b3da094038d0255df02184ea5/DearDough.md#dd-517--a-competing-listener-hypothesis-was-exercised-only-on-a-quiet-port).
It added no duplicate. An independent review of the execution proof and an
isolated candidate replay both identified that lesson. The replay also
proposed three additional first reports and a matched repeat. These were
prospective recording decisions. Later triage and downstream effort were
unobserved. The live review’s five tool-call envelopes over roughly 42 seconds
included other wrap-up work. The replay took eight calls and 89 seconds with
different context. Neither measure isolates attributable writer effort.

Both Pygardon reviews skipped process retrospective under the project default.
They supply no observed first-report decisions or effort pair. Terry closed
Doughnut’s sample after its first completion. Both temporary notices were
withdrawn. [Doughnut withdrawal](https://github.com/nerds-odd-e/doughnut/commit/c449d1d2202c67ad956fcbd1b32797c15845078f) ·
[Pygardon withdrawal](https://github.com/terryyin/pygardon/commit/4d9470aef6e07fbd82a2869cbaad2dcdc780b732)

In the nine exploratory historical replays, both arms made the same admission
decisions. The candidate admitted the break-check practice by naming a
possible use for the ODF-147 response. A routine entry later pruned in ordinary
triage would still have been written. That gives no basis for crediting fewer
routine first reports, even though the replay showed no consequential
omission in those packets. The reconstructed inputs and incomplete cost record
limit this result to replay. [Historical replay and ordinary-work evidence](https://github.com/terryyin/open-dough/tree/f2ba360abd58a882f84df2c8d0a1f2c3a2fc3802/.planning/slice-plans/709-assess-new-retrospective-findings-pilot)

A future comparison would need an ordinary process review confirmed to have
used the candidate, a baseline review of the same execution-time material,
and measured work through the next reader’s triage and omission check.
