# SEED-005: Improve guidance from a real client observation

## Idea

Use an existing issue or conversation to share one concrete observation from
client use, including the relevant guidance and release version. Review the
problem manually and improve or delete the shared guidance as appropriate.

## Value and evaluation

The reporting user tries the revised guidance on the original task and can
judge whether it helps. Keep the resulting behavior in the guidance itself.

## When to surface

A client observation is useful enough to act on. Refine and estimate that
specific improvement when it appears. The client chooses what to share.
