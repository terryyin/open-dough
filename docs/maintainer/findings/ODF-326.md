## ODF-326 — Unchanged source-only reviews repeat per slice

**Meaning:** The mandatory per-slice source-only review repeats unchanged inputs; available repeat and reuse observations do not settle its marginal benefit or independence.

**Follow-up:** Response implemented by `505561f7`, first released in `v0.3.73`; ordinary outcome pending, not resolved.

**Identity and limits:** New concrete issue at the supplied execution provenance. Related symptoms elsewhere do not establish a common cause. Repeat cost and two reuse observations are retained; independence and net benefit remain unassessed. No instruction to skip the existing review is authorized. Current maintained guidance assessed: `f274a3d0c729504bf8ce00614bf3cd236b7e088b` (source through 0.3.71); this is not substituted execution provenance.
