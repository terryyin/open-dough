## ODF-483 — Preparation accepts a slices heading execution cannot read

**Meaning:** A ready plan can use a singular or descriptive slices heading that the obligations reader refuses before the first delegation.

**Follow-up:** Open, unqueued.

**References and limits:** Related reader-boundary failures [ODF-431](ODF-431.md) and [ODF-456](ODF-456.md) concern slice numbering and slice status respectively.

### Retained evidence — Open Dough / DD-482 (2026-11-13)

**Source mapping:** [Open Dough / DD-482](https://github.com/terryyin/open-dough/blob/07407e12b40115862205041f03e27851b361cfb2/DearDough.md#L540).

A Take-ready plan headed its only slice under `## Slice`. The obligations / plan-progress reader accepts only `## Slices` or `## Ordered slices`, so the first list refused until the coordinator renamed the heading. Matching uncertainty: same reader contract as ODF-368, but the plan had a near-miss heading rather than slices nested under Current decisions with no section.
