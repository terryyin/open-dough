  - The final story measures the complete group including preparation against the recorded baseline, with roughly one minute at two workers as the campaign target.
- **Settled baselines and proof-method context (`SEED-413#speed-intent-source-and-story-reading` Setup cleanup, Evaluation; `#acceptance-shared-by-the-eight-stories` Bullets 1–4).**
  - `SEED-301` provides settled typed reading data, including `anIntentLocationsAnswer`.
