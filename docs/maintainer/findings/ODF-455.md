## ODF-455 — CI incomplete reports omit evidenced same-ref supersession

**Meaning:** A same-ref cancelled CI run reached the coordinator as unexplained incomplete.

**Follow-up:** Open, unqueued.

**Identity and limits:** Open Dough / DD-449 → ODF-455. Two executions verified same-ref cancellation and continued without repair. ODF-289 shows why a newer push alone cannot prove the reason for cancellation. Maintained source assessed at `cf1ea1debbd50eac9cfe73a2e6a6782597764fdf`.

[Original source](https://github.com/terryyin/open-dough/blob/cf1ea1debbd50eac9cfe73a2e6a6782597764fdf/DearDough.md).

after the same observer had registered the superseding push. The coordinator
had to inspect the run and workflow before it could ignore proven supersession.
