## ODF-403 — Additive branch records keep freshness in conflict after trunk text is absorbed

**Meaning:** After a Story Branch takes trunk's amended story or plan text, the three-way freshness check still reports `conflict` / `overlapping-edit` because branch-only execution records (Status, accepted proof, obligations or learnings) sit in or beside trunk-rewritten hunks while the comparison base stays at Take; coordinators strip and restore or relocate records, and one bypassed the required conflict stop.

**Follow-up:** Open, unqueued; not resolved.

**Identity / assessment (2026-11-03):** Two projects, two executions (Open Dough 0.3.81, Clipboard 0.3.82), both using the 0.3.73 freshness response of ODF-354. Distinct from ODF-383 (deletion-only amendment remap) and ODF-404 (a genuine substantive concurrent rewrite). The bypass is an adverse effect of a false conflict, not a missing detection. Maintained guidance assessed at `325f9de8a1f635a44b0071cf0660a2593a21f6b9`.
