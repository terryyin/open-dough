# Launch editing by touch

Story and **Start session** dialogs keep the instruction and selected choices
while the window rotates or its visible height changes. In a short window,
the multiline instruction scrolls inside a shorter field; the dialog's choices
scroll above the effects, **Cancel**, and **Start**, which stay in view. Touch
users can tap session and option labels, and **Startup recovery** actions use
the same comfortable control height. Rotation and editing send nothing;
**Cancel** abandons the dialog, and only explicit **Start** submits its draft.
An accepted startup still reports progress until its native result is known;
an uncertain launch keeps its existing check and continuation advice.

The local Chromium journeys exercise phone/tablet touch viewports, rotation,
and 300px visible height with disposable launches. They establish browser UI
behavior under those conditions, without claiming physical iOS keyboard or
remote-connectivity validation.

[Launch start](LAUNCH-START.md) owns the launch and recovery contract.
