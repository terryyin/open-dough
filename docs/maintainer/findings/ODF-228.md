The [AGENTS.md behavior review](../../../AGENTS.md#behavior-review) walked the
retained cases through those entry points; it reviewed instructions, not native
agent compliance. For plan 310, the planned eight-worker scope is known, while
the timing of individual load snapshots is not established. The rule requires
current target evidence or qualified unknowns before launch. After an overloaded
failure, the same attempt cannot repeat unchanged: its owner can reduce
concurrency without dropping coverage, sequence owned work or defer the exact
whole observation until conditions change. Later-only load or idle-runner
discoveries inform the next decision, not the original launch. Neither the
historical four-worker choice nor load 25–257 becomes a universal policy.

Plan 315 still requires diagnosis and repair of its independent reporter-fixture
race; the later isolated watcher pass cannot clear the failed broad verdict.
Plan 314's accepted unchanged proof stays reusable, with supplementary missing
proof qualified. The [overlap review at ODF-209](ODF-209.md#source-response-and-bounded-review-2026-10-21)
follows delegated, refactor and repair ownership. A healthy target can proceed
after a brief relevant inspection; inaccessible remote-runner headroom stays
unknown and local measurements cannot supply it. Deferral stops only dependent
proof and retains its owner and next condition while independent work continues.

Useful outcome: the guidance now requires a decision before costly work and a
changed basis before an overloaded repeat, preserving strict verdicts. Added
burden is a concise existing handoff and a recheck when its basis changes, rather
than a host registry or routine approval. This review demonstrates that those
decisions are expressible and consistent; it adds no occurrence or resolution.
