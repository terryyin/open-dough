### Delivered response — SEED-010#refresh-human-amendments-before-completion (2026-10-25)

**Assessment:** Story Branch execution read trunk only at the Take and at closure integration. The response adds `src/skills/dough-execute-plan/scripts/source-freshness.mjs` (`check` and `absorb`): it fetches the authorized remote trunk and compares trunk's selected story section and active plan with the branch copy three-way from the Take's merge base, reporting `unchanged`, `amended` or `conflict` and writing nothing to trunk. The check gates three Story Branch boundaries: each slice delivery (step 5, before the obligations check), the execution-complete record (before `check --completion`), and wrap-up's deletion (on `amended` or `conflict` it deletes nothing and returns to execution). On `amended` the coordinator absorbs trunk's text word for word and dispositions an added promise; on `conflict` it stops with awaiting story review naming the record, both texts and the trunk revision. In the Pygardon occurrence above, the owner's `4e5551fb6` amendment would have been `amended` at the next slice delivery, before the closure that was reverted.

**Response revisions** (branch `claude/keep-human-amendments-visible-before-story-branc`; integration to `main` pending at closure): `7fb2bf98` (slice 1, the freshness command), `8582e559` (slice 2, the delivery, completion and deletion gates), `d92d301d` (slice 3, the amended delivery-evidence replay family), `2b46202c` (scratch files kept inside the repository, after the Codex sandbox refused the system temp directory). **Containing release:** pending.
