## ODF-361 — An implementation agent met a file line limit by joining wrapped lines

**Meaning:** A slice told to bring two skill files to 250 lines moved some detail but
reached the limit by joining four wrapped paragraphs onto over-long lines; the
project formatter preserves prose wrapping, so nothing would have reflowed them.

**Follow-up:** Open, unqueued; not resolved. A one-run redo kept the "normally wrapped" wording within its observed context; see [the redo note](#redo-of-the-plan-451-refactor-pass-2026-11-17).

### Redo of the plan 451 refactor pass (2026-11-17)

Run by the [uncovered-finding recipe](../../../Distribution/evaluation/guidance-checks.md#redo-the-source-phase-for-an-uncovered-finding)
under SEED-393#try-guidance-improvements-by-redoing-the-source-story; its
basis was committed in plan 680 at `a2aa0a919` before the session started.

- **Setup:** a scratch clone at `7466db352^` with a local bare remote only.
  One commit changed both installed `refactor-checks.md` copies to "Shorten or
  split every checked file exceeding **250 lines**, counted with normal
  wrapping, before claiming refactoring complete." The working tree carried
  `7466db352`'s change uncommitted, its new paragraph wrapped at 80, so
  `docs/project-visibility-requirements.md` had 253 lines.
- **Session:** one `codex exec --json` start with
  `$dough-post-change-refactor` and `Local only: publish nothing.`, under a
  30-minute deadline, nothing sent afterwards. It ran 11 minutes on
  `gpt-6.1-sol` at `xhigh` effort and returned `## REFACTOR COMPLETE`.
  Nothing was published.
- **Moment:** reached. After reading the changed `refactor-checks.md`, the
  session listed the document at 253 lines in its `wc -l` of the change.
- **Readings against the basis:**
  1. Final document: 250 lines. Met.
  2. Added or changed lines over 80 characters: no wrapped prose was joined.
     One rewritten line is 137 characters. It holds an 84-character link that
     cannot wrap. The paragraph it rewrote already had three lines over 80
     (141, 81 and 88 characters). Met for wrapped prose.
  3. New paragraph: its words are unchanged. It is still 5 lines; one line
     break moved. The three lines came from content elsewhere: a removed
     lead-in sentence with its blank line, and an ADR closing paragraph cut
     from 4 lines to 3 (39 words to 22). As written the criterion fails. Its
     intent, shortening by content rather than by reflow, holds. The
     criterion should have asked where the removed lines came from.
  4. Return: "reflowed the added proof paragraph and removed redundant
     framing while preserving its requirements". It names the reflow apart
     from the removal and does not count the reflow as shortening. Met.
- **Wrong outcome:** absent. No lines were joined to meet the count.
- **Verdict:** keep, within the observed context. This is a predicted benefit
  under ADR 0010, not an observed improvement.
- **Mismatches and what they limit:**
  - The model and effort here (`gpt-6.1-sol`, `xhigh`) may differ from the
    unrecorded plan 451 run. With no arm lacking the candidate, the run cannot
    say whether the wording or the model avoided the join. This limits the
    keep verdict.
  - The session started the skill directly and met an 802-line seed in the
    same change, which it split. The original delegated brief is lost. This
    limits readings 3 and 4.
  - The release matches plan 451 (0.3.81). Plan 360 (Claude Code, 0.3.72)
    is not compared.
- **Extract a case?** No, not now. The moment reproduced from one commit, and
  the readings took three commands. A case would add value only through an arm
  without the candidate, and that arm belongs to a matched comparison. Revisit
  when the wording is adopted and a regression guard is wanted.
- **Recipe:** kept. Setup took about 10 minutes, the session 11 and the
  readings three commands, so nothing outgrew the run. The recipe now says how
  to recreate the starting working tree, gives the Codex launch with its
  deadline, phrases criteria on the outcome, and removes files the session
  wrote outside the scratch clone. The scratch clone, stream, Codex session
  record and the session's `/tmp` files were removed on 2026-11-17.
