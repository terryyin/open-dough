## ODF-494 — Formatted-size proof measures the candidate before formatting

**Meaning:** A physical line-count bound is accepted from an intermediate representation. The formatter changes the count and guarded delivery has to refuse before the candidate is revised.

**Follow-up:** Open, unqueued.

**References and limits:** Related to [ODF-309](ODF-309.md), where required build proof itself rejects pre-format input. Here the shape measured by acceptance changes at the formatter boundary.
