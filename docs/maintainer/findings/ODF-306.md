## ODF-306 — A native probe ends a pending one-time consent

**Meaning:** A delegated native probe terminates the requesting process before a human answers first-use system consent, leaving Denied state and requiring manual recovery.

- **Follow-up:** Open, unqueued.
- **References / identity:** Open Dough / DD-323 → ODF-306. Distinct from ODF-262's split permission/callback probes: the failed boundary is ending a still-pending human consent.
