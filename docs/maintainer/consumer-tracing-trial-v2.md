for [ODF-150](findings/ODF-150.md), applying Accepted
[ADR 0010 — Definition of improvement](../adrs/0010-definition-of-improvement-accepted.md).

## Decision and comparison

Reject this compact candidate and retain current published practice. The fixed
replay triggered the preregistered B-only missed-purpose/lost-lead stop, so
prospective recruitment never activated. There are **zero prospective cases**
and no seven-day later-effect exposure. This is one repeated fixed case, not
population evidence or an effectiveness rate. The four-case comparison remains
unperformed. No automatic adoption, ODF retirement or new occurrence follows.

The beneficiary is the developer avoiding missed effects and later repair.
A is the full current handoff; B adds a compact effect → responsibility →
observing assertion → proof/gap navigator over the same frozen evidence.
The predeclared gain required no B misses or lost A discoveries, at least 20%
and one minute lower median review time, and at least 10% lower total mechanism
effort across four complete audited prospective pairs. This replay fails the
quality stop and its paired timings fall short of those benefit thresholds;
it cannot supply the required medians or later-use observations.
