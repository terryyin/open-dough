# Intent Coverage follow-up measurement

Compare the next window of wrap-up checks with the 12–14 November 2026
baseline after limiting automatic checks to stories that removed or rewrote
tests, or whose proof was questioned. Count reports that lead to landed
proof, worker and coordinator share, and skipped stories with a later
regression that the check would have caught.

The baseline had 37 reports, three leading to maintained proof that landed.
Median elapsed time was 4.8 minutes. Workers used 0.97% of Claude Code spend
and 0.75% of Codex tokens in the sample. Attributable coordinator turns were
bounded above by $19.68 and 1.3% of Codex tokens. Cursor had no local token
measurement. [Full baseline](https://github.com/terryyin/open-dough/blob/ffdd3dd800a7325237adcfc9c54920956bb63f7b/docs/maintainer/intent-coverage-cost-research.md#value-of-automatic-checks-1214-november-2026)
