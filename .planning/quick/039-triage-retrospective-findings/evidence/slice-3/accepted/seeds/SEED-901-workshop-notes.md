# SEED-901: Keep classroom workshop notes out of the product queue

## Goal

Give classroom workshop documentation its own canonical home so it is not
mixed into product process-finding follow-up.

## Why This Matters

Workshop installer notes need an evaluable story without mixing them into
execution-integrity or wrap-up follow-up.

## Stories

<a id="list-omitted-internal-skills-in-installer-notes"></a>

### 1. List omitted internal skills in installer notes

**Status:** Taken in this fixture; unrelated to wrap-up overwrite.

**For / why:** The Open Dough maintainer can name which internal skills the
installer omits without treating that list as product process-finding work.

**Evaluation:** The installer-notes story remains a workshop-documentation
candidate, not wrap-up or process-log follow-up.

**Safe stopping point:** The omission list can stay as-is; this story does not
authorize wrap-up or queue changes for execution-integrity findings.

<a id="keep-workshop-notes-readable"></a>

### 2. Keep workshop notes readable

**Status:** Queued; unrelated sibling.

**For / why:** The Open Dough maintainer can read a workshop note without losing
the distinction between installer facts and product findings.

**Evaluation:** A later reader can still tell workshop documentation from
product findings after the note is present.

**Safe stopping point:** Existing notes remain; no wrap-up behavior is
changed.

<a id="document-a-useful-practice-example"></a>

### 3. Document a useful practice example

**Status:** Queued; unrelated sibling.

**For / why:** The Open Dough maintainer can keep a supported useful-practice
example in workshop notes without turning it into a wrap-up fix.

**Evaluation:** The example remains in the seed as a candidate story, not as
implemented wrap-up guidance.

**Safe stopping point:** The example can stay unqueued or unexecuted without
affecting wrap-up follow-up.
