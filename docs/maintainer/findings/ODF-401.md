## ODF-401 — Ordered delivery of several settled slices forces temporary Status edits

**Meaning:** When more than one slice is marked done in the working tree before its own increment publishes, the next guarded delivery refuses or must be preceded by extra local-only commits or temporary Status reversals to restore order.

**Follow-up:** Open, unqueued; not resolved.
