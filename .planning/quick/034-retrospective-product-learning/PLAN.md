# Turn execution learning into product backlog decisions

## Source and outcome

[SEED-010 Story 6](../../seeds/SEED-010-learn-from-execution-retrospectives.md#turn-execution-learning-into-product-backlog-decisions).
Source complete 2026-09-10. Proposed skill:
[`dough-execution-retrospective`](../../../src/skills/dough-execution-retrospective/SKILL.md).
Behavior evidence:
[`RECOGNITION.md`](../../../src/skills/dough-execution-retrospective/RECOGNITION.md)
and [evidence/](evidence/). Story 2 owns
[release and adoption](../../seeds/SEED-010-learn-from-execution-retrospectives.md#use-released-retrospective-log).

The developer receives implementation, process, and product review of one
execution. Each enabled review questions alignment with the existing
near-future direction without proposing or editing that text. Product learning
produces recommendations or authorized backlog maintenance. Evidence and
hypotheses stay distinct. Historically approved work is not a defect merely
because today's direction differs.

**Excluded:** DearDough.md writing, token instrumentation, automatic guidance
rewrites, discovery/decomposition of new ideas, full-backlog audits,
implementation of findings, and release/adoption. Cross-tool verification was
skipped by human direction.
