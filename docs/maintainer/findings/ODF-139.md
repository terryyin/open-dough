## ODF-139 — Reported defect dismissed without checking the story

A slice can satisfy a narrower plan and still miss the agreed outcome.
Pygardon plan 412 accepted held and ordered symbols appearing in history when
the story required them in the exclusions list. In Doughnut’s later baseline
comparison, the coordinator caught a narrowing before delegation and corrected
the plan to compare all 24 rows.

The acceptance decision uses the complete selected story, human amendments
and observations of the delivered behavior. The historical comparison found
both caught and missed omissions. The frequency, cost and model contribution
remain open.
