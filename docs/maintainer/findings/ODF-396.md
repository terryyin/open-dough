## ODF-396 — Source-derived consumer terms flood module-move listings

**Meaning:** Automatically derived generic import/path/symbol terms in an ordinary module move produce roughly a hundred noisy consumer matches and repeated dispositions.

**Follow-up:** Open, unqueued; not resolved.

**Identity / assessment (2026-11-01):** Uncertain relationship retained separately from ODF-350 broad supplied terms and ODF-352 changed data keys. The derivation source differs; no shared cause or missed consumer is established. It is an independent cost of current tracing, not a reason to remove indirect proof.
