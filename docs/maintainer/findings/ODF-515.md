## ODF-515 — Records-only planned completion lacks a documented guarded context

**Meaning.** Once every slice is done, the completion reference calls increment delivery without specifying the plan and already-completed slice required by the fresh planned operation. Supplying the last done slice allowed the affected completion publication to proceed.

**Follow-up.** Open and unqueued.
