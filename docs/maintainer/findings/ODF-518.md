## ODF-518 — Formatted-line planning samples miss construction shapes that wrap

**Meaning.** The planner measures one formatted test family and generalizes to sites with different call shapes. Longer terms or fact-method chains wrap under the formatter and exceed the agreed per-case size.

**Follow-up.** Open and unqueued.

### Pygardon / DD-527

[Source at the reviewed revision](https://github.com/terryyin/pygardon/blob/ef988e251b32f764b2c9852ccd78c956b8e08d73/DearDough.md#dd-527--a-formatted-line-premise-measured-on-one-line-sites-was-applied-to-multi-line-sites).

Keep the two distinct executions and the two site shapes. The first source expresses date-only precision. The second timestamp locates the accepted commit just after the return. Related to ODF-512’s unrepresentative sizing samples; line wrapping and context capacity have distinct observed consequences.

A plan settled its line-budget premise by formatting one spec whose converted
calls all fit on one line, then applied the chosen call form to every site.
Sites whose terms spanned lines wrapped the builder chain under the formatter
and grew, which the owner's per-case no-growth rule forbids.
