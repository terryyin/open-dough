## ODF-466 — A merged tree’s check result is recorded without running it on the merged change

**Meaning:** After a trunk merge brings a converted export, a record claims a static check passed on the merged tree that the check actually rejects, or a staged-only hook omits the unchanged consumer, so a long suite finds the defect.

**Follow-up:** Open, unqueued.

**Identity and limits:** Open Dough / DD-474 → ODF-466. Uncertain relationship to ODF-357 (recorded results contradicted by their own commands) and ODF-463 (merge proof selection). Two executions. Maintained source assessed at `220026a4e1b8409ffa81f50cd8e3130945ff8472` (0.3.93).
